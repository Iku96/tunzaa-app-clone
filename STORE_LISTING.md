## Full Description

Afrizon is your all-in-one marketplace platform connecting buyers, and vendors. Whether you're shopping for quality products or running a business, Afrizon makes it seamless.

**For Buyers:**
Browse thousands of products from local vendors, enjoy fast delivery, and track your orders in real-time. Shop with confidence and support local businesses.

**For Vendors:**
Set up your online store, manage inventory, process orders, and grow your business with built-in tools designed for success.

**For Delivery Partners:**
Earn on your schedule by accepting delivery requests, navigating with ease, and getting paid promptly for completed deliveries.

**Key Features:**
• Browse and search products from local vendors
• Secure payment options including mobile money and cash on delivery
• Real-time order tracking and delivery updates
• Multi-language support (English, Swahili, French)
• Easy vendor store management
• Flexible delivery partner dashboard
• In-app messaging between buyers and vendors
• Order history and saved addresses
• Wishlist and product recommendations

Join thousands of users transforming local commerce with Afrizon.
